Items

Items

Guide

Items are what you carry in your inventory, and a great many of them place as blocks once you put them down. Most stack to 64; a few, including ender pearls, snowballs, eggs and signs, stack to 16; and tools, weapons, armour, potions and buckets take a whole slot each.

Tools
Pickaxes break stone and ore, axes break wood, and shovels break dirt, sand and gravel. Hoes turn dirt and grass into farmland, and are also the fastest tool for leaves, hay bales, moss and sculk.
Using the right tool is much faster, and many blocks drop nothing at all unless you break them with the correct one.
The tiers run wood, stone, copper, iron, diamond and finally netherite. Copper, added in 1.21.9, sits beside stone rather than above it: it shares stone's mining level, so it unlocks nothing new, but it mines faster, lasts longer at 190 uses against stone's 131, and takes enchantments better.
Gold is the outlier. It mines faster than any other material but wears out almost immediately, and it is limited to the same blocks as wood, so a golden pickaxe cannot even mine gold ore.
Each tier mines everything the tier beneath it can and more: stone and copper unlock iron ore, copper ore and lapis; iron unlocks gold, redstone, emerald and diamond; and diamond unlocks obsidian and ancient debris. Netherite mines nothing new, but it is faster and far more durable than diamond.

Food
Food restores hunger and, more importantly, saturation, which controls how long you stay full. Cooked meats and golden carrots have high saturation; bread and raw food drain away quickly.
Always cook meat. It restores far more, and raw chicken carries a real chance of food poisoning. Rotten flesh, spider eyes and pufferfish are worse again, and are better kept for brewing than for eating.

Potions
Brewing needs a brewing stand, blaze powder as fuel, and water bottles. Nether wart makes the awkward potion that almost every recipe starts from.
Redstone extends a potion's duration, glowstone strengthens its effect at the cost of that duration, gunpowder turns it into a throwable splash potion, and dragon's breath turns a splash potion into a lingering cloud.

Utility Items
Buckets carry water, lava, milk or fish. A water bucket is the standard answer to a long fall, and milk clears every status effect, good and bad alike.
Maps and compasses keep you oriented. A compass points to your world spawn rather than to your bed, unless you bind it to a lodestone.
Every ender chest opens the same inventory, and that inventory belongs to you alone, so nothing you leave in one can be taken by another player. Shulker boxes keep their contents when broken, which makes them the only real solution to moving a base.
Elytra and firework rockets are the fastest way to cross open ground, though travelling through the Nether still covers more Overworld distance for the same effort.
Name tags stop a mob despawning, which is the only way to keep a particular mob for good.

Durability and Repair
Every tool has a durability bar, and the item breaks once it runs out.
Combining two damaged items of the same type repairs them. Doing it in the crafting grid destroys the enchantments; doing it on an anvil keeps them.
An anvil can also repair with the raw material, iron ingots for an iron pickaxe, and can rename items. Every anvil use raises that item's prior work penalty, though, until the anvil eventually refuses the operation.
Mending is the long-term answer: it repairs an item automatically from the experience you collect, so a tool carrying it never needs an anvil again.
