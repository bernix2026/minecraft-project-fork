Combat Tactics

Combat Tactics

Medium

Once the basics are automatic, combat becomes a question of choosing the right tool and the right enchantment for the mob in front of you. This guide covers ranged weapons, the enchantments that matter, potions, and how to handle the mobs that punish a naive approach.

The Sweep Attack
A fully charged sword swing made while standing on the ground hits every mob in a small arc in front of you. It does not trigger while sprinting or jumping, which is a common reason players never see it. The Sweeping Edge enchantment increases the damage the secondary targets take.

Bows
Hold to draw. A full draw takes about a second and gives maximum damage and range; a partial draw is much weaker and drops off quickly. The enchantments worth having are Power for damage, Punch for knockback, Flame for burning, and Infinity so a single arrow lasts forever. Note that Infinity and Mending cannot coexist on the same bow.

Crossbows
Slower to load, but a crossbow stays loaded, so you can prepare a shot before a fight starts. Multishot fires three arrows at once, Piercing lets arrows pass through several mobs and through shields, and Quick Charge cuts the reload time.

Choosing Enchantments
Sharpness, Smite and Bane of Arthropods are mutually exclusive, so pick deliberately. Sharpness helps against everything. Smite is far stronger against undead, which means zombies, skeletons, drowned, husks and the Wither. Bane of Arthropods covers spiders and silverfish.
On armour, Protection is the general choice, while Blast, Projectile and Fire Protection are situational. Unbreaking and Mending are worth having on everything you intend to keep.

Potions
Strength raises melee damage, Healing and Regeneration keep you standing, Fire Resistance makes the Nether survivable, and Swiftness helps you disengage. Splash potions apply to an area, which is how you heal yourself mid-fight without stopping. Lingering potions leave a cloud, useful for choke points.

Golden Apples
An ordinary golden apple gives Regeneration and Absorption, which is often the difference in a fight that went wrong. The enchanted version is far stronger and worth saving for boss fights and raids.

Mob-Specific Tactics
Endermen: do not look at the head. If one turns hostile, stand under a two-block ceiling so it cannot reach you, or step into water.
Skeletons: close the gap or shield-walk. At range they will win.
Witches: they drink potions to heal, so burst them down rather than trading slowly.
Pillagers: raise a shield against the crossbows and close in.
Phantoms: they only appear when you have not slept for several nights. Sleeping is the counter.
Drowned with tridents: break line of sight or shield, as a thrown trident hits hard.

Positioning
A sprint attack knocks a mob backwards. Use it to keep creepers at a safe distance, to push mobs off ledges, and to buy yourself the moment you need for the cooldown bar to refill. Controlling distance is most of intermediate combat.
